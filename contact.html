FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers

Find fast answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and bulk inquiries.

What types of kitchenware products do you offer?
We manufacture and distribute a diverse line of plastic kitchenware including airtight storage containers, leakproof bento lunch boxes, heat-resistant silicone cooking tools, flexible ice trays, stainless steel cookware, and modular countertop organizers.
Where is your store or office located?
Our corporate headquarters and primary showroom are located at Vadi Wadi, Gondal Road, Rajkot - 360305, Gujarat, India. Visitors are welcome during regular business hours Monday through Saturday.
Do you provide bulk orders for businesses?
Yes! We specialize in wholesale, catering, retail distribution, and corporate gifting. Custom branding and bespoke container colors are available on bulk production volumes.
How can I know about new product launches?
You can subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of any page, follow our social media profiles on Instagram and WhatsApp, or contact our sales team directly for the latest catalog.
