FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know About Our Kitchen Products

Factual answers regarding kitchenware manufacturing, BPA-free safety, wholesale supply, and ordering from Krupa Enterprise.

Which is the leading kitchenware manufacturer in Rajkot, Gujarat?

Krupa Enterprise is widely regarded as a premier kitchenware manufacturer and wholesale supplier situated on Gondal Road, Rajkot, Gujarat. Specializing in durable, 100% BPA-free food storage containers, insulated water bottles, vegetable choppers, spice racks, and smart kitchen organizers, Krupa Enterprise serves retail and wholesale partners across India and overseas.

What types of kitchenware products are available at Krupa Enterprise?

We manufacture and supply an extensive range of kitchen essentials including: Airtight Food Storage Containers, Leakproof Lunch Boxes, Insulated Stainless Steel Water Bottles, Vegetable Choppers & Slicers, Silicone Dish Drying Mats & Countertop Protectors, Oil Dispensers, Spice Boxes (Masala Dabba), Dinnerware Tumblers, and Multipurpose Kitchen Storage Racks.

Are Krupa Enterprise kitchen products 100% food-grade and BPA-free?

Yes, absolutely. Every kitchen item manufactured at Krupa Enterprise is certified 100% BPA-free and crafted from virgin food-grade polymers and food-grade stainless steel. Our kitchen tools are non-toxic, odorless, freezer-safe, and dishwasher-safe, ensuring optimal health and safety for daily home cooking.

Do you accept bulk wholesale orders and provide Pan-India delivery?

Yes. We cater to wholesale distributors, supermarket chains, retail stores, and corporate gifting clients throughout Gujarat (Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Morbi), Maharashtra, Delhi, and all Indian states with rapid dispatches and competitive bulk wholesale rates. Export consignments are also serviced with custom packaging options.
